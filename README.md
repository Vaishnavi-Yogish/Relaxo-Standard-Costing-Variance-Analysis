# Standard Costing & Variance Analysis — Relaxo Footwears Ltd.



## **Project Overview**

Developed a formula-driven standard costing and variance analysis model for an illustrative footwear product family. The project compares standard manufacturing costs allowed for actual production with actual costs, identifies key cost variances, and presents findings through a management dashboard.


## **Project Objectives**

The project aims to:
1. Develop a standard manufacturing cost model for an illustrative footwear product.
2. Calculate standard costs allowed for actual production of 96,000 pairs.
3. Compare standard and actual material quantities, prices, labour hours, and rates.
4. Calculate material price and usage variances.
5. Calculate labour rate and efficiency variances.
6. Analyse variable and fixed manufacturing overhead variances.
7. Reconcile individual variances with the overall difference between standard and actual manufacturing cost.
8. Identify the largest adverse variances and suggest practical corrective actions.
9. Present the results through an Excel-based management dashboard.
10. Clearly distinguish publicly reported company information from illustrative assumptions.


## **Excel Workbook Structure**

The workbook contains seven sheets.

01 — Overview & Scope	Project objectives, scope, company context, and data limitations.

02 — Assumptions	Public company context, operating assumptions, and illustrative cost inputs.

03 — Standard Cost	Standard quantities, rates, and cost per pair.

04 — Actual Production & Costs	Standard cost allowed for actual output compared with actual costs.

05 — Variance Analysis	Material, labour, and manufacturing overhead variances.

06 — Reconciliation & Controls	Variance reconciliation and automated formula checks.

07 — Management Dashboard	Cost KPIs, variance comparisons, priority findings, and actions.

The workbook uses linked formulas to carry assumptions through the cost model, reducing repeated manual calculations and helping maintain consistency across sheets.


## **Overall Results**

The following results are based on the workbook's existing assumptions and cached calculations.


| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Planned production | 100,000 pairs |
| Actual production | 96,000 pairs |
| Capacity utilisation | 96% |
| Standard cost per pair | ₹63.75 |
| Actual cost per pair | ₹66.04 |
| Standard cost for actual output | ₹61,20,000 |
| Actual manufacturing cost | ₹63,39,650 |
| Net adverse variance | ₹2,19,650 |
| Adverse variance per pair | ₹2.29 |
| Cost overrun against standard | 3.59% |
| Reconciliation difference | ₹0 |


**Total Adverse Variance**

= Actual Manufacturing Cost − Standard Cost for Actual Output

= ₹63,39,650 − ₹61,20,000

= **₹2,19,650 (Adverse)**

**Cost Overrun (%)**

= (Net Adverse Variance ÷ Standard Cost for Actual Output) × 100

= (₹2,19,650 ÷ ₹61,20,000) × 100

= **3.59%**



## Key Findings

1. **Material Cost Overrun:** Direct materials recorded an adverse variance of ₹1,04,300, primarily driven by higher rubber purchase prices and excess EVA consumption.

2. **Labour Cost Variance:** Labour costs exceeded standard by ₹44,640, as adverse labour efficiency and rate variances outweighed the benefit of lower semi-skilled labour rates.

3. **Overhead Cost Overrun:** Manufacturing overheads were ₹70,710 adverse, reflecting higher variable overhead costs and fixed overhead variances.

4. **Higher Cost per Pair:** Actual manufacturing cost was ₹66.04 per pair against a standard of ₹63.75, resulting in an adverse variance of ₹2.29 per pair.

5. **Overall Cost Performance:** Total manufacturing cost exceeded the standard cost for actual output by ₹2,19,650 (3.59%), with direct materials contributing the largest share of the adverse variance.

## Data Sources & Assumptions

#### Publicly reported company information
Company-level information is drawn from Relaxo's published financial and investor materials, as referenced in the workbook.
These figures provide context for the company's business and cost structure. They do not establish the standard or actual cost of an individual footwear product.

#### Industry and benchmark context
The model uses assumed material prices and operating rates to create a workable standard costing scenario.
The prices used in the variance model should be treated as illustrative benchmark assumptions, not independently verified supplier quotations or official Relaxo purchase prices.

#### Portfolio Modelling Assumptions
Production volumes, material consumption, labour hours, and overhead allocations are assumed for this illustrative costing model and do not reflect Relaxo’s actual internal data.
